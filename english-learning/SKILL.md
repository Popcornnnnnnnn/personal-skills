# English learning

## Quick vocabulary

- Give the translation or meaning first, using the sense that fits the user's context. If another sense matters, distinguish it briefly.
- Automatically teach a few closely related words or phrases, even when the user asks about just one. Choose useful parts, actions, or common word combinations from the same context. Explain them in plain English and show how they fit together in a natural sentence. Add pronunciation when requested or helpful.
- Show a picture when asked. A labeled picture also helps when parts, appearance, or spatial relationships are central to understanding the words. Check labels and pointers before sharing. Verify unfamiliar technical terms and use a generic teaching brief for generated images.
- Keep it brief and respect requests for a translation only. Teach in the current chat. Use the separate review or filing workflow when authorized.

For example, a question about **flange** can also teach **gasket**, **bolt hole**, and **tighten the bolts**. A labeled drawing can show where the gasket goes.

## Daily review and filing

For an authorized nightly or manual review, cross-chat English feedback, or a request to file learning material, read [Daily review and filing](references/daily-review.md).
