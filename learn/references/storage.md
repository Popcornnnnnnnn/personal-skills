# Local evidence and publication contract

Use a private local store selected by the user. The author's default is `~/.local/share/workflow-notes`; actual Notion destinations and owner context stay in the local profile. The store is not uploaded or synchronized by this package.

Keep pending proposals separate from canonical records. Store immutable approved revisions and sanitized evidence by content hash, with a current pointer per canonical case. Keep exact approval evidence and derived write/readback receipts separately. Do not mirror whole conversations, hidden reasoning or credential objects.

The optional `record-schema.json` and `scripts/validate_store.py` define a concrete JSON store interface. Preserve source identities, actual actor/model provenance when observable, supported facts, counterevidence and limits. A knowledge note can have no executed task; do not invent an error, actor, success or human nomination to fill fields.

Interactive writes bind a real human approval to the exact reviewed payload and action scope. A separately authorized historical batch additionally needs its fixed window, human assignment, task index and qualifying primary evidence. No preauthorized batch or destination ships with this public package. Existing archival exceptions must be explicitly configured and stay outside the public contract.

Notion can serve as an editable reader/feedback surface after verifying its actual workspace/schema. Preserve existing page IDs, human changes and comments, and keep operational analysis out of the reader's routine fields. A historical immutable payload does not override newer authorized presentation rules. Tools cannot independently prove human authorship or grant write authority.
