# Acceptance and bounded repair

Compare the intended run/account/folder scope with observed records and final outputs. Count accessible, read, archived, removed, invalid and retained items separately. Keep raw receipts out of reader-facing prose.

A run can finish with unresolved items. A saved article does not prove full source coverage, a source-cleanup click does not prove the final state, and a local receipt is not independent remote verification. Read back exactly what the completion claim requires.

Repair only an authorized, understood failure. Preserve original evidence and human decisions; do not restart an entire successful batch, change schedules, install dependencies or alter accounts to make a dashboard green. If no actionable step remains, state the specific remaining limit and recovery condition.
