# Shared writing conventions

Read the installed `notion-writing` skill before authorized Notion changes, including direct API/UI operations. Its local owner profile supplies private destinations and channel-specific rules.

Keep source summaries complete and readable; preserve existing human fields, IDs, comments, children and media. Saving a source is separate from human reading/interest and source cleanup. Use stable source identity to avoid duplicates. A display adjustment preserves data and does not authorize a migration.

For substantive prose, use an installed humanizer where available without losing facts, sources or uncertainty. Put operational receipts in separate local records. Verify the affected content and parent after writing, and use rendered UI evidence when acceptance depends on layout.
